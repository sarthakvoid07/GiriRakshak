# `src/pages/` — Screens

One owner per screen. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

| Screen | Owner | Purpose |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `Login` | **F1** | Sign in as a resident or a district official. |
| `FieldReport` | **F1** | Submit a geotagged report with a photo. |
| `MyReports` | **F1** | A reporter's own submissions and their status. |
| `RiskMap` | **F2** | All zones on a map, coloured by `riskLevel`. |
| `ZoneDetail` | **F2** | One zone — score, rainfall, slope, road status, nearby reports. |
| `DistrictDashboard` | **F3** | Official's queue: verify and action incoming reports. |
